    Season words you’ll hear

    The same few words come up again and again. Learn these and the guides get much easier.

    Pick note
    The list the warehouse uses to pick an order off the shelves.
    Confirm
    Enter what was actually sent or received, so invoices and stock are right.
    Post
    Send something into the accounts. Once posted it is final and has to be reversed to undo.
    Credit note
    A document that gives money back to a customer, or from a supplier.
    Allocation
    Matching a payment to the invoices it is paying off.
    Lodgement
    A batch of customer payments banked together.
    GRN
    Goods Received Note: the record of what arrived from a supplier.
    Nominal
    The account codes in the books that every transaction is recorded against.
    Journal
    A manual entry in the accounts, used for adjustments and corrections.
    Month end
    Closing a month so the next one starts clean.
    Consignee
    A delivery address for a customer, if different from their main one.
    Sort key / market code
    A label used to group customers or products for searching and reports.
